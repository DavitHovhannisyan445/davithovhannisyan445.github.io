Current work

Droplet

Invisible watermarking for AI content provenance and AI agent safety

Droplet is developed by a team of three co-founders. It embeds an invisible watermark into images after generation, so it works with any generative model. Its blind detector identifies watermarked images without access to the original, even after common transformations such as compression, resizing and cropping.

What we have now

  • Blind zero-bit watermark detector trained with structured hard-negative learning (paper accepted, see below)
  • Robustness testing against common image transformations

What we're researching next

  • Multi-bit watermark carrying a compact, tamper-resistant Droplet ID
  • Robustness to regeneration by other generative models
  • AgentTrace: binding content to the AI agent that produced it, so anyone who receives the content can find out which agent made it and whether it was within the agent's authorized mandate. This gives operators the evidence to investigate whether the agent was compromised (for example, through prompt injection) or simply made a mistake.
